About Heal One World

Founded in 2009, powered by volunteers.

Heal One World is a Los Angeles 501(c)(3) offering free and donation-based wellness classes. Our volunteer instructors share yoga, Reiki, tai chi, and more, so anyone can explore what supports their body, mind, and spirit.

“Our greatest tools for healing lie within our own selves.”

Volunteer

Share your time and skills.

Heal One World runs entirely on volunteers, from trained instructors to people who simply want to help. Whatever you bring, there’s a place for it.

Become a volunteer

  • Teach a class

    Share yoga, Reiki, tai chi, meditation, or another practice.

  • Host a workshop

    Lead a one-time session on something you know well.

  • Help at events

    Welcome guests, set up, and support the film festival.

  • Intern

    Build real nonprofit experience alongside our team.
